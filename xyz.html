Booking answers

A few things worth knowing.

Clear answers before your trip is the simplest way to make booking feel easy.

Are toll taxes included in the fare?

Yes. The estimated fare includes state highway toll fees, fuel and driver allowance. If a special route or custom pickup changes the fare, we confirm it with you first.

Are all cars air conditioned?

Yes. Every listed vehicle is air conditioned. On steep hill roads, a driver may briefly adjust the air conditioning when it is necessary for safe vehicle performance.

Can I request a luggage carrier?

Yes. Let us know while booking and we will arrange one without an extra charge, subject to availability.

Can I book from Dehradun back to Delhi?

Absolutely. Select Dehradun as your pickup and Delhi / NCR as your drop-off in the estimator, then send the request through WhatsApp.
